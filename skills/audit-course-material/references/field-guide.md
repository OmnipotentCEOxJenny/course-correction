# Field-aware checking guide

Applies to courses, tests, quizzes, workbooks, answer keys and study guides at every level: K-12, college and graduate, vocational and trade, corporate training, and professional licensing and certification prep.

**Always check the current version and its date.** Rules, rates, limits, codes and exam outlines change, often every year. Record the effective date of every source used. If the material was written for an older version, say which one and flag the differences as `outdated`.

| Field | What to check | Primary or authoritative sources |
|---|---|---|
| Math and statistics | Every calculation, units, rounding, formula statements, worked examples, answer keys | Recompute twice by different methods; NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions; the original paper for named results |
| Physical and life sciences | Constants, units, named laws, data values, outdated models | NIST CODATA constants; IUPAC; SI brochure (BIPM); peer-reviewed papers; government science agencies (NASA, NOAA, USGS, NIH) |
| Engineering and IT | Standards numbers and editions, commands, API behavior, version-specific steps | Standards bodies (ISO, IEEE, IETF RFCs, W3C, ANSI, NFPA); vendor official docs for the stated version; certification body exam objectives (CompTIA, Cisco, AWS, Microsoft) |
| Medicine and nursing (NCLEX and others) | Drug doses, lab ranges, guidelines, contraindications, outdated practice | FDA labels (DailyMed); CDC, NIH and WHO guidance; professional society guidelines with date; NCSBN test plan for NCLEX scope; peer-reviewed literature (PubMed). Expert confirmation required. |
| Law (bar prep, paralegal, civics) | Statute and rule citations, holdings, jurisdiction, amendments, repealed law | Official codes and session laws; court opinions from court sites or government repositories; Federal Register and eCFR; state bar and NCBE outlines for scope. Expert confirmation required. |
| Finance and securities (SIE, Series 7, 63, 65, 66) | Rule numbers, contribution and dollar limits, margin and settlement rules, registration requirements, tax figures | FINRA rulebook and FINRA exam content outlines; SEC rules (eCFR Title 17) and SEC releases; NASAA for 63, 65, 66 and the Uniform Securities Act; MSRB rules; IRS publications for tax limits (check the tax year). Expert confirmation required. |
| Accounting (CPA) | Standards references, journal entries, tax figures, audit standards | FASB Accounting Standards Codification; GASB; PCAOB and AICPA standards; IRS publications and the Internal Revenue Code; AICPA Blueprints for scope |
| Insurance and real estate | State-specific rules, license requirements, contract terms, loan limits | State insurance and real estate commission statutes and rules; NAIC model laws; HUD, CFPB, FHFA (current-year loan limits) |
| Trades and vocational | Code editions, safety limits, measurements, procedures | The adopted code edition (NEC/NFPA 70, IBC, IPC, IRC); OSHA regulations (29 CFR); manufacturer specifications |
| Corporate and compliance training | Policy and regulation references, figures, dated claims | The cited regulation or law; regulator guidance; the organization's current official policy |
| History, social studies, civics | Dates, names, quotations, attributions, causal claims | Primary documents and archives (national archives, Library of Congress); official records; peer-reviewed history |
| Language arts and writing | Grammar rules stated as absolute, quotations, attributions, examples | Major style guides (state edition); the original text for quotations; reputable dictionaries |
| Economics and business | Data values, dates, definitions, model statements | Government statistics (BLS, BEA, Census, Federal Reserve, World Bank, IMF); peer-reviewed papers |
| Software tutorials (any app) | Menu paths, shortcuts, setting states across steps, version | Vendor manual for the same version; reproducing the steps when possible |

## Licensing and certification caution
Do not reproduce copyrighted or confidential exam questions or proprietary prep material. Audit what the user supplies, quote only what is needed to show an error, and cite public sources. Use the official exam outline only to judge scope.

## High-stakes caution
For medical, legal and financial content, the report is a review aid, not professional advice. Every flag in these fields carries "needs expert confirmation".
